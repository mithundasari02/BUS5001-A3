# Q1(e) — Demonstration video script (target ≤ 1:50)

Audience: senior executives. Tone: business value first, demo second, close on next steps.
Record in Zoom/Teams/Stream recording with screen-share of the Dialogflow CX Simulator.

## Shot list

| Time | On screen | Say (verbatim, ~180 wpm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Slide or agent welcome screen | "Hi, I'm [name], and this is a quick preview of our proposed ESG Sustainability Assistant — a chatbot that turns ad-hoc staff reports into structured, trackable sustainability incidents." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Slide: business problem bullets (three items: mis-routed reports, incomplete data, no structured ESG data feed) | "Today, issues like leaks or waste contamination arrive by email or phone, so they get miss-routed, lose detail, and never make it into our ESG reporting in a usable form." |
| 0:25–1:20 | Screen: Dialogflow CX Simulator | "Let me show you how it works. Say: *there's a water leak in Building C, level 2.* (run turn 1–2). The bot classifies it, captures location, description, and the key judgment call — urgency. If it's urgent — say *same day* — it confirms the full record and logs it with a reference. Then I'll show escalation: when the reporter says there's a safety risk, the bot interrupts, routes to the Urgent Response team and gives a Security call fallback — so automation never blocks an emergency." |
| 1:20–1:40 | Screen: flow diagram (`flow-diagram.png`) | "Under the hood, it's a mapped-focus flow: five intents, slot-filling for four parameters, two fallbacks, and an escalation branch. Because every conversation produces structured data, this feeds our facilities ticketing system and ESG dashboard conceptually — no new manual work." |
| 1:40–1:50 | Slide: value + next steps | "For the business: faster response, complete and clean ESG data, and a consistent staff experience. Next step: two-week pilot with the Facilities team." |
